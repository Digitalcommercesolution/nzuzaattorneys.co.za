Understanding Your Rights

How long does an RAF claim take?

Road Accident Fund claims can take anywhere from 18 months to 3 years or more, depending on the complexity of the medical assessments and the litigation required to reach a settlement.

What is a 'No Win, No Fee' agreement?

A 'No Win, No Fee' or contingency agreement means that our firm only takes payment if we successfully win your case or secure a settlement. This ensures that expert legal representation is accessible to everyone.

What documents do I need for a divorce?

Typically, you will need your marriage certificate, identity documents, children's birth certificates, and proof of residence. During your consultation, we will provide a detailed checklist based on your specific case.

How do I report a deceased estate?

A deceased estate must be reported to the Master of the High Court within 14 days of the death. Our firm provides expert assistance in reporting and administering these estates efficiently.

Can I sue for medical negligence?

Yes, if you have suffered harm due to a medical professional's failure to provide the standard of care expected. We conduct thorough investigations to build strong legal cases for medical malpractice claims.
