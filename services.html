RAF CLAIMS

Road Accident Fund claims for personal injury or loss of support - FOREWORD

If you or a loved one has been injured in a road accident, you may be entitled to compensation from the Road Accident Fund (RAF). Navigating the RAF claim process can be complex and time-consuming. At Nzuza Attorneys Incorporated, we have the expertise to handle your claim and ensure that you receive the maximum compensation you are entitled to.

Our RAF claim services include:

  • Claiming for funeral benefits and loss of support if a family member passed on
  • Instituting loss of earnings and medical expense claims
  • Assessing the merits of your claim
  • Gathering medical records and evidence
  • Lodging claims with the Road Accident fund
  • Litigation against the RAF
  • Negotiating settlements

We take on most RAF claims on a contingency fee basis (No win, No Fee), meaning you only pay us if we successfully secure compensation for you. This ensures that you have access to expert legal representation regardless of your financial situation.
